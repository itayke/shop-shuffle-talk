Shop Shuffle talk · sketch 3

Clips drop in like new shops

Each clip falls from high above the screen and lands on the street with the game's squash, framed like the lobby's QR card. Its title floats in the sky as a banner, and the text under it is set like the lobby's tagline. Press → to step between two slides, as a clicker would.

How it moves

  • The fall speeds up the way a joining shop's does, over the same time, but starts far above the screen: the clip is out of sight while the video loads and comes in fast.
  • The landing squashes the clip by the game's own amount and plays its land sound. No bounce.
  • The text washes in once the clip has landed, so the eye goes to the footage first. The title is a banner; the line under it has no outline and wraps, so it can carry more words.

Still to decide

  • Title colors. The game uses gold for READY?, green for WALK-INS! and blue for PARACHUTES!. All titles are gold here; the colors could also mark the talk's sections.
  • The words. Both lines are placeholders. Over a 16:9 clip the sky fits about one line of text; more needs a smaller clip.
  • The exit. For now the old slide just disappears. It could also be pushed off by the next one.
